Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Seven days to change your mind, no questions asked. Cancel whenever you want. Here are the details.

The seven-day refund

Ask for a full refund within 7 days of any charge and you'll get it. You don't have to explain why.

Email support@talkova.app from the address on your account. We process refunds within 2 business days, and the money reaches your card 5 to 10 business days after that, depending on your bank.

After seven days

We don't refund past that point as a rule, since the Free plan lets you try Talkova before paying anything.

The exception is when something went wrong on our end — the service was down for an extended period, you were charged twice, or a paid feature didn't work and we couldn't fix it. Write to us and we'll make it right.

Canceling

Cancel any time. There's no minimum term, no cancellation fee, and nobody will ask you to stay.

Email support@talkova.app and we'll cancel it for you.

What happens next:

  • You keep your paid features until the end of the period you already paid for
  • After that, your account moves to the Free plan
  • Your account isn't deleted, and neither is your practice history
  • You can subscribe again whenever you want

Canceling stops future charges. It doesn't refund the current period — for that, use the seven-day window above.

Changing plans

Moving up (Pro to Premium) takes effect right away. Stripe charges the difference for the days left in the month.

Moving down (Premium to Pro) takes effect at your next billing date. You keep Premium features until then.

Failed payments

If your card is declined, Stripe retries over several days and emails you each time. If it still doesn't go through, your account moves to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted — update your card and pick up where you left off.

If we shut down

If Talkova ever discontinues service, we'll give you 30 days' notice, refund any unused paid time, and let you export your practice history.

Chargebacks

If something's wrong, email us first. We answer within 24 hours and refunds inside the seven-day window are automatic — it's faster than a chargeback and it doesn't cost either of us anything.

Accounts with an open chargeback are suspended until it resolves.

Getting in touch

Refunds, cancellations, or billing questions: support@talkova.app

We answer within 24 hours, in English or Spanish.
